🥧 Pie Charts

Represent data using sectors and percentages.

📖 Learn the Concept

A pie chart is a circular graph divided into sectors. Each sector represents a part of the whole data.

Formula: Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100

Angle: Angle = (Value ÷ Total) × 360°
