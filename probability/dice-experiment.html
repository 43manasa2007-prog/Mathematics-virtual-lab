📖 Learn the Concept

A standard die has six equally likely outcomes: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.

Probability of getting any particular number:
P(number) = 1 ÷ 6

So the theoretical probability of rolling a 4, for example, is:
1 ÷ 6 ≈ 16.67%
