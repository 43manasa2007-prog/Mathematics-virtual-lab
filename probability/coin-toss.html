📖 Learn the Concept

A fair coin has two possible outcomes: Heads and Tails.

Probability of Heads: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 = 50%

Probability of Tails: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 = 50%

For a fair coin, both outcomes have an equal theoretical probability.
