Explore probability using a four-section spinner.

📖 Learn the Concept

This spinner has four equal sections: Red, Blue, Green and Yellow. Each section has the same theoretical probability.

Number of equal sections: 4

Probability of each color:
1 ÷ 4 = 0.25 = 25%

When the spinner is spun many times, the experimental probabilities should get closer to the theoretical probability.
