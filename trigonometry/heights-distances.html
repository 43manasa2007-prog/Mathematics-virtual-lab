📐 Heights & Distances

Use trigonometry to calculate heights and distances.

📖 Learn the Concept

Heights and distances can be calculated using the tangent ratio in a right-angled triangle.

tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent
Height = Distance × tan θ
Distance = Height ÷ tan θ
