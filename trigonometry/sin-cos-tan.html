📖 Trigonometric Formulas

For a right-angled triangle, the three basic trigonometric ratios are:

sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse
cos θ = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent
