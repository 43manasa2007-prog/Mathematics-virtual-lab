# 📐 Mathematics Virtual Lab

An interactive web-based Mathematics Virtual Lab designed to help students learn and explore mathematical concepts through interactive experiments, visual representations, graphs, and practical activities.

The project brings different areas of mathematics together in one platform and follows a learning-by-doing approach to make mathematical concepts easier to understand and visualize.

---

## 🎯 About the Project

Mathematics Virtual Lab is an educational web application developed to provide students with an interactive environment for exploring mathematical concepts.

Instead of learning mathematics only through formulas and theoretical explanations, the platform allows students to experiment with values, observe results, explore graphs, and understand mathematical relationships visually.

The virtual laboratory currently covers:

- Geometry
- Algebra
- Trigonometry
- Statistics
- Probability

## 📚 Mathematics Modules

### 📐 Geometry Lab

The Geometry Lab provides interactive activities for understanding fundamental geometric concepts.

Experiments include:

- Pythagoras Theorem
- Circle
- Triangle
- Geometry concepts

---

### ➗ Algebra Lab

The Algebra Lab helps students explore equations, expressions, factorization, and algebraic graphs.

Experiments include:

- Linear Equations
- Quadratic Equations
- Factorization
- Algebraic Graphs

---

### 🎲 Probability Lab

The Probability Lab provides simple virtual experiments for understanding probability through random events.

Experiments include:

- Coin Toss
- Dice Experiment
- Random Number
- Spinner

---

### 📊 Statistics Lab

The Statistics Lab introduces students to basic statistical concepts and data visualization.

Experiments include:

- Mean, Median and Mode
- Bar Graphs
- Histograms
- Pie Charts

---

### 📐 Trigonometry Lab

The Trigonometry Lab helps students understand fundamental trigonometric ratios, functions, and applications.

Experiments include:

- Sin, Cos and Tan
- Heights and Distances
- Trigonometry Graphs
